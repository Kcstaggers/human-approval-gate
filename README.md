# Human Approval Gate Kit

Version 1.0.1

Published: August 23, 2026

The Human Approval Gate Kit helps leaders, educators, operators, and small teams define what a person must check before AI-assisted work can affect a real decision or action.

It replaces vague instructions such as "a human will review it" with five observable controls:

- **Consequence held:** no consequential action occurs before approval.
- **Listed reviewer:** a named, qualified person owns the decision.
- **Evidence checked:** the reviewer examines defined evidence, not polished output alone.
- **Allowed outcomes:** accept, revise, reject, and escalate remain available.
- **Record and recovery:** the decision, exact version, reason, and safe response are recorded.

## Responsible-use boundary

The Human Approval Gate is a proposed practical method. It is not validated research, a standard, a certification, legal advice, clinical guidance, or a guarantee.

Adapt the kit to the laws, policies, contracts, professional duties, privacy rules, security controls, accessibility requirements, and records obligations that apply to the actual work. Every included example is synthetic. Do not use patient, employee, employer, client, confidential, or restricted information to demonstrate the kit.
